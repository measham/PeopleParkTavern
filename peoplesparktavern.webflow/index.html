about us

Nestled in the heart of Victoria park –

Our secret garden is the east London escape you’ve been looking for. With one of the largest beer gardens in London, you won’t want to be anywhere else.

The People's Park Tavern is more than just a pub—it's an experience. With our on-site brewery, BBQ kitchen, pop-up cocktail bar, and plenty more, we promise the best night out in East London. Inspired by the people and for the people.
